Boeing Aircraft Safety Trends

Aggregate analysis of real NTSB accident/incident records, 1982–2022

Real data, aggregated. Source: NTSB Aviation Accident Database & Synopses (public domain). This covers all Boeing aircraft models in the database — not only the 737 — across every era present in the data, and includes everything the NTSB classifies from minor incidents to fatal accidents. Every number below is an aggregate count or rate; no individual event narratives, names, or identifying details are shown.
